# My First Annotated Game

## The Opening

The game started with the Ruy Lopez.

The first few moves were:

**1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bb5**

The important idea here is that White develops the
king's knight while putting pressure on the e5-pawn.

---

## Move 5 — A Decision

After **5...a6**, White has to decide what to do with
the bishop.

I chose:

**6. Ba4**

This keeps the pressure on the knight.

### Question

What would you play here?

Think about the position before continuing.

---

## What I learned

One thing I learned from this game was the importance
of asking what my opponent wants to achieve.

Before every move, I try to ask:

1. What is my opponent threatening?
2. Are any of my pieces hanging?
3. What candidate moves do I have?
4. What happens after my opponent's best response?
